## Verified OK (independent recomputation, not using `evi` for the checked quantity)

1. **Sign and closure (item 1).** I recomputed ΔV for 6 events from the `.mat` file with my own trapezoid integration: 4 random events plus the max- and min-`dV_upper` events (TV20120125, TV20080408, TV20141024, TV20120420, TV20091019/TV20111212, TV20090225). I used both the pairwise window and the fixed window, at HAT = 1.75 and 2.14. Results agree to ≤ 0.001 m³/m. The residual comes from my exact-crossing triangles against A's clipped-sample trapezoid. ΔV > 0 means erosion everywhere: events, schema, `legacy` (`V = Int[:-1] − Int[1:]`) and the audit. V_pre − ΔV_fixed = V_post holds to 0.0 for all 6. Chain closure V_pre(i) − ΔV(i) = V_pre(i+1) holds with 0 violations over all 128 v1 intervals and 0 over the v2 intervals. `dV_upper` = `dV_upper_fixed` (max diff 1e-13), confirming the window-independence claim.
2. **HAT split (item 2).** Recomputed for 6 events at 1.75 and 2.14 m (above). `landward_crossing` returns the landward-most crossing on a synthetic barred profile: 85.0 m expected, 85.0 m returned. 0 profiles have more than one crossing of 1.75 or 2.14 m. Profile #5 crosses +3 m twice, consistent with the audit's "1/168 at +3 m or +1.75 m".
3. **Clock fix (item 3).** I checked it independently. CSV stamps fall at 02, 05 … 23 h (first stamp 2007-12-31 23:00). With +3600 s, Hs matches the raw WW3 field `ww3.201001_hs.nc` at node (44.6667 N, −1.5 E) with max |diff| 8.9e-16 over 248 steps; offsets of −1, 0 and +2 h give no matching timestamps. The `setup` column matches MARS2D `XE` (`Niveaux_mars_2010_1.nc`) with max |diff| 4.4e-16 and corr 1.000 at +3600 s, against corr 0.876 / 0.968 / 0.968 / 0.875 at 0 / +30 / +90 / +120 min. All 168 survey datenums have fraction 0.5, so surveys sit at 12:00 UTC.
4. **No double counting (item 3).** Intervals are contiguous (`survey_end[k] == survey_start[k+1]`). Σ per-interval storm hours = 9,939 h equals storm hours in the series over [first survey, last survey). Per-interval `n_storms`, `storm_hours_total`, `n_clusters` (72 h rule, recomputed), `storm_straddles_survey`, `days_since_last_storm` (past-only) and `E_Hs2dt_storm` all match my reimplementation in 128/128 intervals.
5. **Storm thresholds (item 4).** Hs95 = 4.2241 m and Hs75 = 2.3740 m on [2009-01-01, 2013-12-01) only. I detect 83 storms, the same as A.
6. **Runup hand calculation (item 5).** Inputs: Hs = 5 m, Tp = 14 s, β = 0.03, h = 50 m. Results: L0 = 306.02 m, kh = 1.2219, Ks = 0.9131, H0 = 5.476 m, ξ₀ = 0.224 < 0.3, R2% = 0.043√(H0L0) = **1.760 m**. Code: H0 = 5.4761, R2 = 1.7603.
